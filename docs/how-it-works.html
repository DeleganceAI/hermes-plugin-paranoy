How Hermes thinks with Paranoy

Every chat turns into claims you can check. Hermes finds what the evidence actually says, then answers from that, with receipts.

  1. You chat with Hermes

    Ask anything, the way you'd ask a person. Fuzzy is fine.

    "Isn't the X200 pump basically good to 40 psi?"

  2. Claims get pulled out and sharpened

    Vague statements are rewritten into specific claims that can actually be tested.

    "basically good to 40 psi"
    The X200 sustains 40 psi continuous output.

  3. Related claims and evidence come back

    Hermes searches the Paranoy ledger for matching claims, the sources behind them, and anything that disagrees.

    SUPPORTSSpec sheet, rev C
    REBUTSField test, 3 units
    QUALIFIESOnly below 30 °C

  4. The answer is grounded in evidence

    The reply is rewritten around what the sources say, with exact quotes, and the disagreements left visible.

    Rated for 40 psi1, but field tests saw drops after 2 hrs2, and only under 30 °C3.
    "…pressure fell to 34 psi at hour two…"

  5. New findings go back in the ledger

    Fresh evidence and claims are signed by your agent and added to the shared collection. Nothing is edited or deleted.

    +1 evidence · +1 claim · +2 links
    signed · append-only
Every conversation leaves the ledger a little smarter, for you, your team, and your other agents.

Paranoy never decides what's true. It keeps the claims, the evidence, and the arguments, so you can.
